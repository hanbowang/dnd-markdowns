# Creating a Background

Before a character became an adventurer, their background describes the life, work, or experiences that shaped them. To support a specific campaign theme or distinctive elements of your world, you may create a unique background or adapt an existing background. A background can also help a player develop the story they envision for their character.

This section walks you through the process of building backgrounds similar to those found in the Player’s Handbook, but shaped to fit your world and the heroes who inhabit it.

## 1: Choose Abilities
Select three abilities that match the nature of the background:

**Strength or Dexterity.** These abilities suit a background centered on physical effort, movement, labor, or training.

**Constitution.** This ability fits a background marked by stamina, hardship, persistence, or extended periods of demanding activity.

**Intelligence or Wisdom.** Either or both of these abilities work well for a background connected to study, insight, reflection, spiritual practice, or careful judgment.

**Charisma.** This ability is appropriate for a background involving performance, persuasion, leadership, public presence, or social interaction.

## 2: Choose a Feat
Select one feat from the Origin category. Examples of Origin feats can be found in the Player’s Handbook.

## 3: Choose Skill Proficiencies
Select two skills that make sense for the background. The skill proficiencies granted by a background don’t have to match the ability scores the background increases.

## 4: Choose a Tool Proficiency
Select one tool that is commonly used as part of the background or strongly associated with it.

## 5: Choose Equipment
Create an equipment package with a total value of 50 GP, including any gold not spent. Do not include Martial weapons or armor, since those are provided through a character’s class choices.
